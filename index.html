      Words used on this board
      four
      Four in a line with one point that would make five. It forces a reply.
      open four
      A four with two completing points. It cannot be blocked, so it wins.
      open three
      A three that becomes an open four if it is left alone, so it has to be answered.
      closed three
      A three blocked on one side. It can still grow into a four.
      fork
      One move making two threats at once. Only one can be answered, so it wins. The usual kinds are four-three, double four and double three.
      overline
      Six or more in a row. It wins under freestyle, is not a win under standard, and is forbidden for Black under renju.
      VCF
      Victory by continuous fours: a forced win made only of fours, since each one must be answered.
      VCT
      Victory by continuous threats, the same idea allowing open threes as well as fours.
      forbidden point
      Renju only. A point Black may not play: double three, double four or overline.
